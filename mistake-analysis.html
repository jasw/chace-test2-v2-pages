Q3(a.iii) Oblique asymptote - 1/4

Question focus: For f(x) = (x^2 - x - 12) / (2x - 15), find a and b where the oblique asymptote is y = ax + b.

What went wrong: Chace attempted polynomial division, but the working did not reach the correct quotient/asymptote.

Correct method: Divide x^2 - x - 12 by 2x - 15. The quotient is (1/2)x + 13/4, so the oblique asymptote is y = (1/2)x + 13/4. Therefore a = 1/2 and b = 13/4.

Error type: Conceptual / procedure not completed.
